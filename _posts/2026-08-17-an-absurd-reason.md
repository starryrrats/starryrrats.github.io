---
title: An Absurd Reason
layout: post
tags: poetry
---

Is this why we live? <br>
For the moments of absurdity? <br>
When we're contemplating death-- <br>
Hello dancing rats, I see you. 

I've been dreaming, <br>
Of all manners of dying. <br> 
The gun or the pill, <br>
No, I'll settle for the pencil. <br>

There's plenty of guilt abound, <br>
So much to go around. <br>
Guilt for drinking, <br>
Guilt for existing.

I want no bother, <br>
But god, I'm so tired, father. <br> 
I'm privileged and selfish, <br>
But please, allow me my wish. <br>

For years I tried <br>
For years I found enough, <br> 
Yet it's not enough now  <br>
And I just want to drown. 

As the episode continues, <br>
While my thoughts are grim, <br>
I welcome the well-timed dancing rat <br> 
And wish I was that.
